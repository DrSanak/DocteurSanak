About me

I am Dr Serdal Sanak, ophthalmic surgeon specialised in refractive surgery (laser correction for myopia, hyperopia, astigmatism) and cataract surgery. I practise in Brussels within the Chirec network.

My dual specialisation covers the whole field of refractive surgery: corneal laser surgery (Femto-LASIK, PRK) on one side, intraocular surgery (ICL, PRELEX, cataract) on the other.

Refractive surgery in Brussels

Refractive surgery encompasses all the procedures that correct visual defects and reduce or eliminate dependence on glasses and contact lenses. Myopia, hyperopia, astigmatism, presbyopia: for each of these conditions, there are today precise, well-documented surgical solutions, adapted to very different profiles.

This website gathers what patients ask me in consultation: how each condition works, the techniques available, recovery, results and risks.

Every eye is different. The choice of technique is made after a precise assessment of your corneal anatomy, your correction and your day-to-day visual needs.
