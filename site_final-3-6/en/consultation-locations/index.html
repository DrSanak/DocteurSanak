Consultation locations

Consultation locations of Dr Serdal Sanak

Dr Serdal Sanak consults at five centres: Auderghem (CHIREC Delta), Uccle (CHIREC Edith Cavell), Ixelles (Parc Léopold, close to Luxembourg station and the European Parliament), Schaerbeek (Europe Lambermont) and Nivelles (Jean Monnet, Walloon Brabant). Each centre receives patients for screening consultations and preoperative exams in refractive and cataract surgery.

The essentials

  • Auderghem: CHIREC Hôpital Delta, Boulevard du Triomphe 201, 1160. Tel. 02 434 81 09.
  • Uccle: CHIREC Edith Cavell Medical Centre, Rue Général Lotz 37, 1180. Tel. 02 434 81 01.
  • Ixelles: Parc Léopold Medical Centre, Rue du Trône 100, 1050 (close to Luxembourg station and the European Parliament). Tel. 02 434 81 03.
  • Schaerbeek: Europe Lambermont Medical Centre, Rue des Pensées 1, 1030. Tel. 02 434 24 11.
  • Nivelles (Walloon Brabant): CHIREC Jean Monnet Medical Centre, Avenue Jean Monnet 12, 1400. Tel. 02 434 79 11.
  • Booking: Rosa platform (rosa.be) or contact@docteursanak.com for preoperative exam requests.
  • Procedures: Femto-LASIK, PRK, ICL, PRELEX, cataract surgery.

The main site, located on the Delta hospital campus in south-east Brussels. Access by metro (line 5, Delta station) and tram (line 8, Delta stop). On-site parking. This is where laser procedures and cataract surgery are performed, in addition to consultations and preoperative exams.

What you will find here

Full technical platform. Preoperative exam, laser surgery (Femto-LASIK, PRK) and intraocular surgery (ICL, PRELEX, cataract).

Booking

How to book an appointment at one of these centres

Online via Rosa. The rosa.be/fr/hp/serdal-sanak platform lets you select a slot at one of the centres based on availability and consultation reason (first visit, preoperative exam, postoperative check-up).

By phone. Each centre has its own number (see above). The secretariat will direct you to an available slot at the centre of your choice.

By email for a cataract or laser preoperative exam. contact@docteursanak.com, with the subject "Cataract exam request" or "Laser exam request". A slot will be offered at the centre best suited to your situation.
